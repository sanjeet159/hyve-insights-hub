Founders often fail at freelancing because they hire individuals instead of vetting skill-sets. Here is the blueprint to get it right.

 
- 1. Technical Audit: Always review previous code or designs, not just resumes.
 
- 2. Communication Test: A 15-minute sync reveals more than a 10-page proposal.
 
- 3. Cultural Alignment: Ensure they understand startup velocity and product-market fit.
