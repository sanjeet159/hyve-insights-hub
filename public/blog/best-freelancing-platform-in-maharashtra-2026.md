### 2. Upwork — The Global Export Standard

For Maharashtra's highly skilled developers and specialized consultants, Upwork is the window to the world. It’s perfect if you are targeting US or European clients. However, the high cost of "Connects" and the effective 15% loss (fee + FX + transfer) makes it less ideal for domestic projects.

### 3. LinkedIn Services — The Networking Hub

In Mumbai, who you know matters as much as what you know. LinkedIn is a powerful tool for MH freelancers to connect directly with decision-makers in Bandra-Kurla Complex (BKC) or Magarpatta. The downside? Zero payment protection unless you use a third-party escrow service like HYVE to handle the billing.

### 4. Fiverr — Productizing Small Gigs

Best for entry-level freelancers in Nashik or Nagpur who want to build a portfolio by doing quick tasks like logo design, social media posts, or simple voiceovers. The 20% commission is high, so it's best as a stepping stone rather than a long-term career platform.

### 5. Toptal — The Elite Tier

If you are a top-tier software engineer in Hinjewadi or a high-end UI designer in Mumbai, Toptal offers access to Fortune 500 clients. The screening process is tough, but the hourly rates are significantly higher than any other platform.
