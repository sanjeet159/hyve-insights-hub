Getting ghosted after submitting your final deliverable is every freelancer's nightmare. In 2026, the industry is moving away from blind trust toward secure, escrow payment models.

Escrow is a neutral third party that holds the client's payment until you have successfully delivered the work. It eliminates the "pay me now" vs "give me work first" debate.

 
- Security: The client commits the funds upfront.
 
- Transparency: Both parties agree on milestones before the work starts.
 
- Speed: Once the work is approved, the funds are released instantly.
