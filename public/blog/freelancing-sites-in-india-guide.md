## How to Choose the Right Freelance Site

- Niche vs. Generalist: General sites (like Upwork) have volume, but niche sites (like Dribbble for design or Toptal for dev) have better quality.

- Payment Protection: Always prioritize platforms that offer escrow services or milestone-based payments.

- Competition Level: High-traffic sites are competitive. Look for platforms that have built-in vetting to reduce the noise.
