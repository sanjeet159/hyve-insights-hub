## Where Fiverr still wins

Fiverr is unbeatable for one thing: productised, repeatable gigs under ₹10K. Logo designs, voiceovers, 30-second video edits, basic SEO audits. The discovery is built in — no bidding, no chasing leads.

The catch: the 20% cut never reduces, and most Indian freelancers cap out around ₹40–60K/month before burning out from the volume game.

## Where Upwork still wins

Upwork is still the strongest platform for global solo hourly work — especially US/EU clients paying $40–100/hour. The Top Rated / Top Rated Plus badges still carry real weight in 2026.

The catch: Connects cost real money, the bidding is exhausting, payouts take a week, and the algorithm increasingly favours US-based freelancers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which platform is most transparent on fees — Fiverr, Upwork or HYVE?

HYVE charges a flat 10% with no hidden withdrawal, Payoneer or FX costs — you always know what you take home. Upwork charges 10% (sliding to 5% on long retainers) plus withdrawal + FX losses. Fiverr charges a flat 20% on every order, regardless of size or repeat client.

### Is HYVE better than Upwork for Indian freelancers?

For Indian startup clients, retainers, team-based projects and same-day payouts, yes. For US/EU global hourly work, Upwork is still stronger because of its international client base.

### Do I need to bid on HYVE like Upwork?

No. HYVE uses a match-based model — projects are matched to vetted freelancers based on skill, availability, and past work. There are no Connects, no bidding wars, and no proposal templates to write.

### How fast does HYVE pay compared to Fiverr and Upwork?

HYVE releases money the same day the client approves the milestone (via escrow). Upwork takes 5–10 days. Fiverr holds funds for 14 days. For cash-flow-sensitive freelancers, this is the single biggest difference.

### Can I use Fiverr, Upwork and HYVE at the same time?

Yes — most full-time Indian freelancers do exactly that. Fiverr for productised top-of-funnel gigs, Upwork for global hourly clients, HYVE as the main home base for Indian startup retainers and team projects.
