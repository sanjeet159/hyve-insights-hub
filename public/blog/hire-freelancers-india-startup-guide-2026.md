## The 5-step hiring process (under 5 days)

 
 DAY 1
 Write a 1-page brief

Outcome, deliverables, deadline, budget range, references. Skip this and you'll get 50 generic pitches.

 

 
 DAY 2
 Shortlist 3 freelancers

Three is the magic number. More than that and you'll never decide. Filter by past work in your industry, not just rating.

 

 
 DAY 3
 15-minute call + paid test

Pay ₹2,000–₹5,000 for a 2-hour scoped task. The test reveals communication, speed and quality in one shot.

 

 
 DAY 4
 Lock scope + milestones in escrow

Split the project into 3–4 milestones. Fund each one in escrow upfront, release on delivery.

 

 
 DAY 5 ★
 Kickoff with a written SOW

One Slack/WhatsApp channel, weekly check-ins, async updates by default. Skip the "quick call" trap.

 

## The 30-minute vetting checklist

 Ask every shortlisted freelancer these 6 questions:

 
 01.Show me 2 past projects most similar to mine.

 02.What was the outcome — not just the deliverable?

 03.How many active clients do you have right now?

 04.What's your typical response time during a project?

 05.Can I speak to one past client for 5 minutes?

 06.What kicks a project off the rails for you?

## 5 hiring mistakes to avoid

 
 01
 Hiring the cheapest bid

The cheapest quote almost always becomes the most expensive project once rework is factored in.

 

 
 02
 Paying 100% upfront

Always milestone-based. 25–30% kickoff, the rest tied to deliverables.

 

 
 03
 No written brief

"You know what we mean" is the most expensive sentence in freelancing.

 

 
 04
 Hiring 1 freelancer for 4 skills

Hire a small team instead. Designer + dev + marketer outperforms any one "full-stack" generalist.

 

 
 05 ★
 Skipping the paid test task

A 2-hour ₹3,000 test saves you a 2-month ₹3,00,000 mistake.
