 MISTAKE 01
 

## Hiring the cheapest option available

 

 
 A startup needs a logo. Three designers quote ₹5,000, ₹18,000, and ₹40,000. They hire the cheapest. Here is what actually happens:

 
 ✗ Designer delivers something generic, then becomes unavailable

 ✗ Second designer hired to fix it — another ₹8,000

 ✗ Mediocre brand needs to be redone in 18 months

 ✗ Total cost: ₹13,000 + time + opportunity cost

 

 
 The real cost formula

 Bad hire = Initial payment + Fix cost + Time lost + Delayed launch. Almost always more than a quality hire would have cost.

 MISTAKE 05
 

## Hiring based on portfolio aesthetics alone

 

 
 A beautiful portfolio is necessary but not sufficient. Ask these 4 questions before hiring anyone:

 
 
 1
 "Walk me through a project that went wrong."

Reveals problem-solving ability better than any portfolio piece.

 

 
 2
 "What information do you need from us to start?"

A freelancer who asks the right questions will deliver the right output.

 

 
 3
 "How do you handle scope changes mid-project?"

Reveals if they have a professional process or just say yes to everything.

 

 
 4
 "Can you share a reference from a previous client?"

Anyone who hesitates here is a red flag.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the biggest mistake startups make when hiring freelancers?

Hiring individual specialists separately and assuming they will coordinate themselves. The handoff gap between designer, developer and marketer is where most timelines and budgets are lost.

### How do I avoid hiring the wrong freelancer?

Ask for outcome-based case studies rather than portfolios, run a small paid trial milestone, and define deliverables in writing before work starts.

### Should startups pay freelancers upfront?

Use escrow-funded milestones instead of blind advances. The money is secured so the freelancer starts confidently, and it is only released when a milestone is approved.
