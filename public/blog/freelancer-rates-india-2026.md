# How to Calculate Freelancer Rates in India for 2026

> Confused about pricing? Learn how to calculate your hourly, daily, or project-based rates as a professional freelancer in India.

Stop guessing your rates. Freelancer rates in India should be based on value delivered, not just time spent.

First, calculate your 'break-even' rate: (Total Monthly Costs) / (Billable Hours). Then, add a premium for your expertise. Never charge based solely on the market floor—charge based on the quality you bring to the table.
