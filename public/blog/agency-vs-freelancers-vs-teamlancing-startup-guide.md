## First, what each model actually means

 
 Agency

 A registered company with full-time employees. You sign a contract, they assign a team, you get one invoice. Layered with account managers, ops, and overhead.

 

 
 Freelancers

 Independent individuals hired one by one — designer, developer, copywriter — each on their own contract, timeline, and bank account. Coordination is yours.

 

 
 Teamlancing

 Pre-formed teams of freelancers who already work together — usually designer + developer + marketer. One brief, one timeline, one escrow, no agency overhead.

## When an agency is the right call

 
- You've raised Series A+ and need predictable invoicing for the board.
 
- The project is regulated — fintech, healthcare, govt — and you need an entity that can sign MSAs and NDAs at scale.
 
- You need 8–15 people working in parallel for 6+ months and the overhead of managing them yourself is unrealistic.

## When solo freelancers are the right call

 
- The job is single-skill and atomic — a logo, one landing page, a 30-second video. One person, in and out.
 
- You already have an in-house lead who will own coordination.
 
- Budget is genuinely under ₹50K and you can't justify anything else.

## When teamlancing is the right call

For 80% of Indian startups between pre-seed and Series A, teamlancing is the answer. Specifically when:

 
- You need multiple skills (design + dev + marketing) to ship one outcome.
 
- Your timeline is 4–12 weeks and you cannot afford agency lead times.
 
- You don't want to be the project manager between 4 freelancers on WhatsApp.
 
- You want milestone-based escrow so nobody runs away with your money or your IP.

## The hidden cost nobody mentions

Every hiring model has a hidden tax that doesn't show up on the invoice:

 
- Agency tax: 30–50% goes to overhead — account managers, sales, ops. You pay senior salaries for junior execution.
 
- Freelancer tax: Your time. 15 hrs/week of coordination at a founder's hourly value = ₹15–30K/week of invisible cost.
 
- Teamlancing tax: Smaller pool. Fewer specialised teams exist (yet). You may wait a few days for the right team to be available.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between an agency, freelancers, and teamlancing?

An agency is a registered company with full-time employees that delivers projects under one contract with high overhead. Freelancers are independent individuals you hire one by one and coordinate yourself. Teamlancing is a newer model where you hire a pre-formed team of freelancers who already work together — you get agency-style delivery at freelancer-level pricing without the overhead.

### Which is cheaper — an agency, freelancers, or teamlancing?

Solo freelancers have the lowest sticker price (₹2–5L for an MVP), but the hidden coordination cost is huge. Teamlancing sits in the middle (₹3–7L) and includes coordination. Agencies are the most expensive (₹8–25L) because you also pay for account managers, ops, and overhead.

### Is teamlancing better than hiring an agency for an early-stage Indian startup?

For most pre-seed to Series A Indian startups, yes. Teamlancing gives you the same coordinated delivery as an agency but ships in 4–12 weeks instead of 14–20, costs 40–60% less, and uses milestone escrow so you only pay for completed work. Agencies make more sense once you're funded and need long-term, large-scale execution.

### When should I hire individual freelancers instead of a team?

Hire individual freelancers when the work is single-skill and self-contained — a logo, one landing page, a single piece of video. If your project needs design + development + marketing working together, individual freelancers create handoff gaps that cost more time and money than they save.
