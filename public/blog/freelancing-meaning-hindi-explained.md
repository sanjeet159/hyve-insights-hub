In simple terms, Freelancing ka matlab hai ki aap kisi ek company ke employee nahi hain, balki aap apna kaam khud karte hain aur alag-alag clients ko services dete hain.

## Benefits of Freelancing

- Aapka time, aapki marzi.

- Multiple clients, multiple income streams.

- Skills improve hoti hain kyunki har project naya hota hai.
