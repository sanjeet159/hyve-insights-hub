## Project Matching

On Upwork, you bid for projects. You write proposals, compete with hundreds of other freelancers, and wait to hear back. The platform's search algorithm rewards established profiles — which means newer freelancers face a steep uphill battle just to get their first client.

On HYVE, matching is different. You create your profile and your team, and HYVE's system matches you to projects based on your skills, experience, and team composition. You receive notifications for projects you are a strong fit for. No bidding wars. No writing the same proposal template fifty times hoping someone notices you.

## Team-Based Work

This is where the comparison diverges most sharply. Upwork is fundamentally built for individual freelancers. You can technically work as an agency on Upwork, but the platform's core experience — profiles, contracts, payment — is designed around individuals.

HYVE is built from the ground up for teams. Every project on HYVE is matched to a freelance team. Clients get a coordinated unit, not a collection of individuals. Freelancers get the benefit of collaboration, skill complementarity, and shared reputation.

## Payment Protection

Both platforms offer escrow, but the experience differs. Upwork's escrow system is robust but designed around USD transactions and international wire transfers. For Indian freelancers dealing with Indian clients, the process is less seamless.

HYVE's escrow is built on Razorpay — India's most trusted payment infrastructure. INR goes in, INR comes out. Milestone approvals release payment instantly to your HYVE wallet. No international payment complications, no waiting days for a bank transfer to clear.

## Vetting and Quality

Upwork is an open platform. Anyone can create a profile and start applying for work. Quality control depends entirely on client reviews, which accumulate over time. New freelancers start with no reviews and struggle to get their first project.

HYVE is a curated platform. Teams go through a vetting process before they can access client projects. This is a higher barrier to entry — but it means every team on HYVE has been assessed, not just self-declared. For clients, this is a significant advantage. For freelancers, it means less competition from unqualified applicants.

## Which Platform Should You Choose?

The honest answer depends on what you need:

 
- Choose Upwork if: You want access to international clients, you are willing to invest time building a profile from scratch, and you are comfortable working in USD.
 
- Choose HYVE if: You are an Indian freelancer or team looking for Indian clients, you want guaranteed payment via escrow in INR, and you prefer to be matched to projects rather than compete for them through bidding.

The two platforms serve different needs. But if you are building a freelance career in India, focused on the Indian startup and business ecosystem, HYVE was built specifically for you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is HYVE better than Upwork for Indian freelancers?

For freelancers serving Indian clients, yes. HYVE pays in INR with no FX loss, integrates with Indian payment rails, matches you to projects (no bidding wars), and is built for teams. Upwork is stronger if you want global clients and are comfortable invoicing in USD.

### Why do Indian freelancers lose money on Upwork?

Earnings are paid in USD, then converted to INR with both an exchange-rate spread and a withdrawal fee. On a steady freelance income, this conversion drag adds up to thousands of rupees lost per year compared to billing directly in INR.

### Do I have to bid for projects on HYVE?

No. HYVE matches projects to teams based on skills, experience, and team composition. You're notified when you're a strong fit — no writing the same proposal template fifty times.

### Can I be on both Upwork and HYVE at the same time?

Yes. Many Indian freelancers use HYVE for Indian clients and team projects, and Upwork for international solo work. They serve different markets and don't conflict.
