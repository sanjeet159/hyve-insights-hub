Starting a tech startup in India in 2026 is easier than ever — and more competitive than ever. AI has compressed build timelines from months to weeks, capital is plentiful for the right deck, and the Indian consumer is finally paying for software.

Here is the honest founder playbook: what to do in your first 90 days, how to spend your first ₹5–10L, and how to avoid the most common ways Indian startups die in year one.

## Phase 1: Validate (Weeks 1–4)

Do not build anything. Talk to 30 potential customers in 4 weeks. Ask about their problem, not your solution.

 
- If you can't find 10 people who say "I'd pay ₹X to solve this today" — your idea isn't ready.
 
- Run a landing-page test: ₹3,000 in Meta ads → measure email signups, not impressions.

## Phase 2: Incorporate (Week 5)

Register a Private Limited Company in India. It costs ₹8K–15K via Razorpay Rize, Vakilsearch, or IndiaFilings and takes 7–15 days.

 
- DPIIT recognition — free, unlocks 3-year income tax holiday + IPR fast-track.
 
- Founders' agreement & ESOP pool — set up 10–15% pool from day one.
 
- Bank account + accounting — Razorpay X / RazorpayPOS, Zoho Books.

## Phase 3: Build the MVP (Weeks 6–14)

In 2026, your MVP should ship in 6–10 weeks, not 6 months. Three honest options:

 
 No-code

 Bubble, Framer, Glide. ₹50K–2L. Ships in 2–4 weeks. Best for marketplaces & internal tools.

 

 
 Freelance team

 Designer + dev + PM. ₹3–7L via HYVE-style teamlancing. Ships in 6–8 weeks.

 

 
 Agency

 ₹10–25L. Ships in 12–16 weeks. Only justified after Series A.

 

## Phase 4: Get to first ₹1L MRR (Months 4–9)

 
- Founders sell. Period. No "growth hire" before ₹3L MRR.
 
- Pick one channel: LinkedIn outbound, Reddit/community, or Meta ads. Master it for 90 days.
 
- Track 1 north-star metric. Everything else is noise.

## Phase 5: Raise (only if you need to)

In 2026, India has 700+ active angel investors and ~120 active seed funds. The best time to raise is when you don't need to. Realistic numbers:

 → Pre-seed: ₹50L–2Cr at ₹8–25Cr valuation. MVP + 3 logos.

 → Seed: ₹3–10Cr at ₹25–80Cr valuation. ₹3L+ MRR, 3x YoY.

 → Series A: ₹40–120Cr. ₹2Cr+ ARR, clear unit economics.

## The 5 things that kill Indian startups in year one

 
- Building before talking to customers.
 
- Hiring full-time too early instead of using freelance teams.
 
- Burning runway on ads before product-market fit.
 
- Co-founder splits without a written agreement.
 
- Raising too much, too early, at a valuation you can't grow into.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much money do I need to start a tech startup in India in 2026?

A lean Indian tech startup can validate, incorporate, and build an MVP for ₹5–10 lakh in 2026 — especially using no-code or teamlancing for the build. Add ₹3–5 lakh for first-year compliance, basic marketing, and runway.

### Should I register a Private Limited Company or LLP for my Indian startup?

Private Limited is the right choice if you plan to raise funding or issue ESOPs. LLPs are cheaper to run but most Indian VCs and angels invest only in Pvt Ltd companies. Register a Pvt Ltd from day one.

### Should I build the MVP myself, hire freelancers, or use an agency?

If you can code, build it yourself or use no-code. If you can't, hire a pre-formed freelance team (teamlancing) — it ships in 6–8 weeks at 40–60% lower cost than an agency, with the same coordination quality.

### When is the right time to raise funding for an Indian startup?

Raise when you have either (a) a working MVP + 3 paying customers or (b) clear early traction (₹3L+ MRR for SaaS, strong retention for consumer). Raising too early at a high valuation is one of the top reasons Indian startups fail at Series A.
